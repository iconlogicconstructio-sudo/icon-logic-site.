The problem

On many construction sites, information is scattered across paper registers, WhatsApp messages and memory. Project managers end up chasing updates, and problems are hard to follow up.

What ICON Logic does

Daily site report
Work done, labour, weather and delays, recorded from a phone.
Materials and equipment
Deliveries, quantities and equipment status.
Issues and actions
Each problem gets an owner and a due date.
Reports for managers
Site information is compiled into PDF reports.

How it works

  • Site staff open one link on their phone.
  • They fill a short form for the day's activity.
  • The information is stored in one place.
  • The project manager gets a report and a list of open issues.

Where we are

Planned later
Worker history from site logs, a client dashboard, supplier prices and a WhatsApp cost estimator.
